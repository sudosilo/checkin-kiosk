Check-In Kiosk

Check-In Kiosk is for a restaurant host stand that wants guests to check themselves in.

The home screen offers walk in or I have a reservation. Walk-ins enter a party size and get an estimated wait and a ticket. Reservations are looked up and confirmed. A staff area behind a PIN shows the waiting list, guest log, and reservations with seat, left, undo, and no-show actions. Data loads and saves through a backend.

Live at checkin-kiosk-bice.vercel.app

This app is checked every morning by Control Troll.
